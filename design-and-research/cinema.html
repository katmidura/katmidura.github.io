Kino Pod Baranami — Redesigning an independent cinema website

Overview
A redesign concept for Kino Pod Baranami's website in Kraków, aimed at improving navigation, readability, and visibility of its full cultural programme alongside ticket purchasing. Designed for cinema-goers, group organizers, and international visitors, the project focused on simplifying how users explore screenings and special events. My responsibilities included conducting interviews, auditing the existing site, co-creating wireframes and high-fidelity screens, running usability tests, and connecting the clickable prototype.
Role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
October 2023 – January 2024 (15 weeks)
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Miro
Team
4 UX/UI Designers
Tags
UX/UI Design, UX Research

01 — Context

More than screenings under one roof

Kino Pod Baranami is a long-standing independent cinema in Kraków whose programme goes beyond standard film screenings to include special events, discussions, workshops, educational initiatives, and online rentals. Its website needed to support both quick ticket bookings and deeper exploration of this cultural offer while keeping the cinema's distinct identity intact.

02 — Problem

Popular tasks worked, everything else was buried

The existing website made everyday browsing difficult due to long text blocks, small text and graphics, inconsistent responsiveness, and a limited English version. Navigation felt unintuitive and repetitive, making key areas like the repertoire and search unclear, while special programmes and events were easy to miss.

03 — Process

Pairing interviews with task observation

Working as a team of four, we conducted roughly 10 individual interviews (about two per member) and think-aloud usability tests on the live site covering tasks such as buying a ticket, finding Baranki w Pieluchach or Filmowy Indeks, locating an online film rental, and checking school programme details. While ticket buying was familiar to participants, locating specific programmes took longer than expected because content was scattered or tucked into unexpected places.

Decision 1 — restructuring around six core sections: We reorganized the information architecture into Repertoire, Buy a ticket, News and events, Education, E-Cinema, and About the cinema so related activities sit together and primary user paths are clear. We highlighted "Buy a ticket" button.

Decision 2 — combining individual proposals into a shared direction: For both low-fidelity wireframes and high-fidelity screens, each team member drafted layouts independently before we came together to merge the strongest elements into a consistent structure.

Decision 3 — keeping a traditional cinema feel with cleaner hierarchy: We paired Inria Sans and League Gothic with a palette of #EDEDED, #ED2024, #757575, and #000000 to modernize readability and layout clarity while referencing traditional cinema aesthetics inspired by the existing identity.

04 — Solution

Clearer paths to films, events, and tickets

Our deliverable is a redesigned website concept covering key pages including the main page, Now showing, Buy ticket, and News and feed, built around clearer navigation and stronger visual hierarchy. I helped develop the wireframes and high-fidelity designs with the team and connected the interactive flows in the Figma clickable prototype. A brief follow-up test on the prototype did not prompt further design changes.

05 — Outcome

A structured redesign concept for future validation

The project produced a reorganized information architecture, visual system, and clickable Figma prototype that bring Kino Pod Baranami's broader programme closer to core browsing and ticketing flows. Because this was a 15-week student project, the redesign was not implemented on the cinema's live site.

Further work would be needed to test the prototype more thoroughly, evaluate event discoverability, expand the English version to match Polish content parity, and verify responsiveness across screen sizes.
